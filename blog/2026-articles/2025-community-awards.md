## 2025 Community Awards
From blockbuster movies that actually got us out to the theater to unexpected indie games that stole our hearts - 2025 proved to be a jam-packed year filled with incredible experiences across our favorite mediums. 

To celebrate, we're introducing a new annual format where we poll the community on their favorite experiences over the course of the year and immortalize them here for us to reflect back on fondly in the future. 

In addition, we'll be highlighting our biggest category - **Community Member of the Year** - to honor and thank those who work to make our corner of the internet so special. 

So without further ado, let's jump into the awards!

## Best Movie or TV Show (TIE!)
### Predator: Badlands
![Key art for the 2025 sci-fi action film Predator: Badlands shows the films primary protagonists Dek and Thia back to back with their weapons at the ready.](https://i.postimg.cc/L5KPsTVy/preadtorbadlands.jpg)

Despite being the seventh installment in the *Predator* franchise, *Predator: Badlands* sets itself apart by being a standalone experience that shakes things by starring Dek, a young Yautja warrior, in the leading role. 

A visual feast with impeccable VFX and fierce cinematic action, *Predator: Badlands* also manages to add metaphoric meat to the bones with a dramatic plot and a *Star Wars* style flair that leaves you invested from the opening scene all the way to the end credits. 

### Weapons
![A screenshot from the 2025 horror film Weapons showing the silhouette of a child running down an empty street in the middle of the night with his arms stretched out by his sides.](https://i.postimg.cc/s1FCqBs3/weapons.webp)

*Weapons* is a mystery horror film that follows a strange case of missing children after seventeen kids from the same classroom get out of bed at 2:17 AM and flee into the night. 

While the film is difficult to discuss without divulging spoilers, it provides a wonderful blend of horror and suspense accompanied by a dash of comedy that left us eager for more. 

Check out Panic Nation's full spoiler-free review of the film [here](https://panicnation.net/blog/weapons-review)!

## Best Tabletop or Trading Card Game
### UNO
![A splash screen for the digital version of the classic UNO card game displays five cards behind the UNO logo with a flashy red background. The cards are a Red 1, Green +2, Yellow 7, Blue reverse, and Draw 4.](https://i.postimg.cc/MpvrVZS3/UNO.jpg)

Step aside Magic, the king refuses to be dethroned. 

An absolute classic that has filled many community members game nights since the early days of the Xbox 360, UNO is a title that continues to dominate play dates thanks to it's simple yet chaotic gameplay. 

Previous community events have ended with members completely depleting the card deck by refusing to play a card and many friendships have been tested as multiple +2s target the same victim over and over. 

While classic and deeper tabletop experiences such as Magic the Gathering, Warhammer 40K, and Dungeons & Dragons have begun to infect more and more members of the community since 2020, with a significant bump in 2025 - UNO is the communitie's favorite card game that simply refuses to die.

## Best Content Creator
### Chon Digital
![Chon Digital's avatar on YouTube. A simple yellow smiley face on a blue background.](https://i.postimg.cc/vmWRx87T/chon.jpg)

Originally focused exclusively on creating video game content, Chon Digital branched out into other topics over the course of 2025 with video essays on various topics like *[Why the Flat Design Era is FINALLY Over](https://youtu.be/WEyFfV_qzE8?si=tjXWYSvB3mdnGOns)*, *[The Disappearance of Third Places](https://youtu.be/NGw_IDEx1jg?si=z3V6Ag7CldIKQM1x)*, and *[How We Came to Hate Technology](https://youtu.be/KUBAm5uynwo?si=4dEhUK2LkvkhNerg)*, which led to a surge in popularity on YouTube. 

Chon is a Gen Z video essayist who creates videos to discuss things on his mind with a witty sense of humor and a whole lot of heart. 

His videos often dive into a multitude of topics and tend to be based on his current interests or thoughts - though they often lean towards disconnecting from algorithms and instead focusing on being human.

While his humor may not connect with everyone, it's clear that his content is created with passion and care and for those who enjoy his creations - it's a no-brainer to chondle that subscribe button.

## Game of the Year (Unlimited & Runner Ups)
### Avowed - Obsidian Entertainment
![The keyart for the 2025 role-playing game Avowed featuring a skeletal warrior with various effects painted across their body.](https://i.postimg.cc/PJb9NS1M/avowed.jpg)

Set in the world of Eora, the same universe as the Pillars of Eternity titles, Avowed is an action role-playing game where players create their own 'godlike', a person who has been blessed (or cursed!) with the physical aspects of a god that chose them in the womb, and set off to the *Living Lands* where they assume the role of an envoy for the Aedyrian Empire and are tasked to investigate a mysterious plague known only as the "Dream Scourge". 

Players are able to cater their equipment to fit their playstyle by using a range of melee weapons, ranged weapons (including guns!), wands, and spell books to help fight their way through the untamed wilderness and discover the truth of what lies within the heart of the *Living Lands*. 

While not a true open-world RPG, *Avowed* instead opts to pack it's unique zones full of life and mysteries to keep the player engaged until the end of their journey - and it's a journey that they won't make alone thanks to the companionship of various characters who can join you on your quest. 

*Avowed* is one of three games released by *Obsidian Entertainment* in 2025 and it's lush beauty and rich lore helps it stand apart from the pack and has left members of the community eager to see what comes next.

## Game of the Year! (TIE!)
### Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 - Sandfall Interactive
![A party of characters from Clair Obscur Expedition 33 stand at the ready to combat the paintress.](https://i.postimg.cc/6pSXk1c3/coe33.jpg)

*Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* lit the world on fire when it released on April 24th, 2025. 

This ambitious, stylish, and highly unique role-playing title was created by a small team and started as a passion project inspired by childhood favorites including the *Final Fantasy* series. 

Upon it's reveal, many people were intrigued but wary of such an ambitious title that seemed to come out of nowhere, but their concerns were quickly laid to rest once the game was playable. 

*Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* impacted a significant portion of the Panic Nation community including those who didn't consider themselves fans of the turn-based RPG genre. 

The characters, story, world, music, and gameplay all meld into a perfect symphony that begs to be heard around the world. 

### PEAK - Aggro Crab & Landfall
![The cover art for the video game PEAK which shows four climbers working together to ascend a mountain.](https://i.postimg.cc/XNyQL1Gs/peak.webp)

Often referred to as the game of the Summer, *PEAK* was an unexpected smash hit when it launched on June 16th, 2025.

This cooperative climbing game supports up to four players who work together to try and ascend a pre-generated mountain while using proximity chat to often hilarious effect. 

Players can find items along their journey to help or hinder their progress and collaboration often gives way to mischief thanks to excellent ragdoll physics and exceptional audio design as your friends screams grow farther away during their sudden descent and likely demise. 

*PEAK* serves as an excellent multiplayer experience that brings the community together for laughs and fun in a time when we need it most. 

### Pokémon Legends: Z-A - Game Freak
![The key art for Pokémon Legends: Z-A shows several trainers and Pokémon engaged in battle during a Z-A battle royale!](https://i.postimg.cc/DZhyNMfQ/legendsza.avif)

*Pokémon Legends: Z-A* takes the traditional Pokémon formula and mixes it up by replacing the turn-based battling mechanics with real-time battles similar to MOBA titles. 

The player sends out their Pokémon and gives commands for attacks by pressing the respective face button which executes the attack and then starts a cooldown timer before the attack can be used again.

This title successfully continues the *Legends* strategy of providing a unique twist on the classic Pokémon formula and tells a story with themes many would consider mature for a Pokémon title. 

With side quests and a fun multiplayer mode, the fun doesn't have to stop once you roll credits, and this game has left some players of the community very excited for the next generation when it hits Nintendo Switch consoles in 2026!

Check out Panic Nation's full review of the game [here](https://panicnation.net/blog/pokemon-legends-za-review)!
